Vanya Maitreya work at QLU sits alongside adult intensives as a space for younger seekers and family-friendly sangha rhythms — storytelling, quiet sitting, and simple seasonal observance during festival months.

This sample magazine notes the strand without publishing private children’s details. Families visiting campus for adult programs often ask how children can share the atmosphere of meditation and care; campus teams guide that case by case.

For public adult offerings, see qluglobal.org and programs.qluglobal.org.
