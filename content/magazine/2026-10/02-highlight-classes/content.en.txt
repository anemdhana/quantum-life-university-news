Highlight offerings reflected on QLU’s public program pages this season:

- Foundational and experiential workshops — short sessions that build a stable inner base, then guided exploration in a held space
- Intensive and advanced intensives — longer processes for participants ready for deeper commitment (including paths toward facilitator readiness)
- Inner Child Healing Intensive — childhood patterns, insight, and a second chance at relating to life; suited for parents, teachers, counselors, and householders
- Past Life Regression (campus, Vikarabad) — residential-style programs with boarding options as announced by Life Foundation / QLU
- Online webinars — for example Awakening Intuition and Sutras of Health (English), exploring clarity, self-healing, and the spirit–mind–body link

Dates and fees change; always confirm on programs.qluglobal.org or info@qluglobal.org before registering.
